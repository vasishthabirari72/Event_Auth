# Draft temporary face-test consent — v0.2

Owner must review this template before using it with volunteers and obtain the planned
review before pilot use. This is for the temporary M1 test, not permanent enrollment.

We are testing whether this laptop can help a volunteer find your member record from
your face. Taking part is optional. You may decline or stop at any time. A member ID
lookup will remain available in the product.

With your permission, we capture three frames to create temporary numerical face
patterns, then capture separate frames for calibration and another round for validation.
People who do not enroll may also consent to test whether the system rejects unknown faces. The images and patterns stay
in this laptop's memory during the test; the tool does not save them or send them to
an internet service. Only aggregate test counts, timing, candidate thresholds, model metadata and laptop
hardware specifications are recorded; the report contains no participant identifiers.

A volunteer checks every proposed identity. This test does not issue any coupons.
For a participant under 18, a parent or guardian must give permission before capture.

Before each enrollment the operator records adult/guardian consent in the temporary
session. A permanent registration consent record with purpose, version, time and
capturing staff ID belongs to M2. Contact/withdrawal details must be supplied by the
organizer in the pilot consent form.
